# Barcelona, Poblenou: a recorded example study

A recorded session of the Assimilator harness's page, replayed in your browser exactly as it was run. No model, no runs.
The question, as it was asked:

> If Poblenou's nine blocks became a superblock, how much more traffic would the streets around them carry, and which streets?

Data and credits:
- Street network, its geometry and its street names: © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License
  (ODbL). The recording, the report and the slides hold OpenStreetMap street names and Poblenou's geometry.
- The car trips: illustrative demand, 3,369 car trips in one morning hour written by rule to show the tools (Barcelona demo,
  Stage 1); not observed traffic, not a forecast of Barcelona's traffic. Its label credits the street network alone.
